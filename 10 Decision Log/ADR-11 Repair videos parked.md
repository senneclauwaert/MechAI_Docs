# ADR-11 Repair videos parked

Back to [Decision Log](Decision%20Log.md)

**Status:** Accepted

**Context:** Repair videos turned into searchable steps were researched and found realistic.

**Decision:** Parked to keep the scope small. Confirmed on 6 November 2026: maybe a few videos later if there is time.

**Why:** The core flow comes first; videos reuse the repair-note flow, which is built in semester 2.

**Alternatives considered:** Building video ingestion in semester 1.

**Consequences:** The research is kept in [Repair videos](../08%20Parked%20and%20Future%20Ideas/Repair%20videos.md).
