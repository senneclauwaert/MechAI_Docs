# ADR-19 Book photos are named by page number

Back to [Decision Log](Decision%20Log.md)

**Status:** Superseded by ADR-29

**Context:** Only a paper handbook was expected, so pages would have been photographed.

**Decision:** Every photo is named after its page number, because the page is the citation.

**Why:** The page number must be right for citations.

**Alternatives considered:** Scanning the whole book.

**Consequences:** Replaced on 6 November 2026: the handbook is a digital PDF. See [ADR-29 Citations come from the digital handbook PDF; pages shown as images](ADR-29%20Citations%20come%20from%20the%20digital%20handbook%20PDF;%20pages%20shown%20as%20images.md).
