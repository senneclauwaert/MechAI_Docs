# ADR-35 Prototype scope: one complete workshop flow for all karts at one venue

Back to [Decision Log](Decision%20Log.md)

**Status:** Accepted

**Context:** December leaves about six weeks of building. The outside reviews advised one complete, measured workflow.

**Decision:** The December prototype (v0.5.0) shows one complete story: PIN login (operator included), the operator reports an issue and marks the kart broken, a mechanic sees the history, asks by voice in Dutch, gets a cited answer with page images and safety levels, logs the repair and takes stock, and rates the answer, with test scores shown. It is built for every kart; kart 07 is only the example. One venue, Brussels, with a venue field for later. Stretch goals: photo issues and the Supercube buttons with fake data. The 3D view moves to semester 2.

**Why:** A complete flow shows the value better than many half-finished features.

**Alternatives considered:** Every feature at a shallow level; a kart-07-only demo.

**Consequences:** See [Planning & Deadlines](../01%20Project/Planning%20&%20Deadlines.md).
