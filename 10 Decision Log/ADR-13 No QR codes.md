# ADR-13 No QR codes

Back to [Decision Log](Decision%20Log.md)

**Status:** Accepted

**Context:** QR or barcode scanning of parts and karts was planned since 5 October.

**Decision:** No QR codes. Karts and parts are picked from a list or found by search.

**Why:** Less to build and label; enough for one fleet of identical karts at one venue.

**Alternatives considered:** QR codes on karts and shelves.

**Consequences:** Confirmed on 6 November 2026.
