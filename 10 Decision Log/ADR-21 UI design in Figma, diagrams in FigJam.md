# ADR-21 UI design in Figma, diagrams in FigJam

Back to [Decision Log](Decision%20Log.md)

**Status:** Superseded by ADR-34

**Context:** Designs and diagrams needed a home.

**Decision:** Screens in Figma, diagrams in FigJam.

**Why:** Visual tools the team knows.
