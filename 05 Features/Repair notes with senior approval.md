# Repair notes with senior approval

Back to [Features](Features.md)

## User story

As a mechanic, I write what I found and fixed; after a senior mechanic approves it, the assistant can show it as a similar past repair.

## What it includes

- A form with kart, symptom, cause, fix, parts and optional photos; text can be spoken.
- States: draft, in review, approved or rejected. A senior approves by re-entering the PIN.
- Editing an approved note removes its approval.
- Approved notes rank below the handbook and are never the source of a safety step.
