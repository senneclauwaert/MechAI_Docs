## Not built

QR or barcode scanning, an outside login service, repair videos, camera stock counting and predictive maintenance are not part of this project. The reasons are in [Parked and future ideas](../08%20Parked%20and%20Future%20Ideas/Parked%20and%20future%20ideas.md) and the [Decision Log](../10%20Decision%20Log/Decision%20Log.md).
