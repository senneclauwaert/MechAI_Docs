# 3D exploded view of the kart

Back to [Features](Features.md)

## User story

As a mechanic, I tap a part group on a 3D model of the kart to see where a part sits, its stock and its handbook page.

## What it includes

- The model can be rotated and zoomed; tapping a group explodes it.
- The part from the answer is highlighted.
- Each part in the 3D model is named after its part number, so tapping it shows stock and the handbook page.
- Built with three.js; the model is made in Blender from simple parts, or from CAD files if Supercube has them.
