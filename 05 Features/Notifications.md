# Notifications

Back to [Features](Features.md)

## User story

As a senior mechanic, I am notified when an issue says "kart unsafe" or a repair note waits for approval; as an admin, when stock is low.

## What it includes

- Events: unsafe issue, note waiting for approval, low stock, task assigned to you.
- A badge inside the app first. Push notifications are limited on iPhone PWAs and are only added if needed.
